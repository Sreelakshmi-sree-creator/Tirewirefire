The studio

Fewer vendors.
Better pictures.

Team Wireframe is a creative studio built around one stubborn idea: the people who imagine the work should be the people who make it. Concept, shoot, build, animate, grade, deliver — under one roof, with one director answerable for the result.

That structure is why a brand film, a photoreal product render and a set of social edits can all come out of the same brief looking like they belong together. No agencies-of-record, no relay of emails between four suppliers, no drift between what was pitched and what was shipped.

We work from Kochi in Kerala and Dubai in the UAE — close enough to shoot on the ground in India or the Gulf, set up to collaborate remotely with teams in Europe, the UK and North America.

How we work

Five stages,
no guesswork.

Every project follows the same spine. Timelines below are typical for a two-to-three minute brand film; CGI-only work usually skips a stage and moves faster.

  1. 01

    Discover

    1–2 days

    A call, a written brief, and an honest conversation about budget and deadline. We come back with scope, approach and a fixed estimate.

  2. 02

    Concept & treatment

    3–5 days

    Written treatment, moodboard and storyboard — the whole film on paper before a rupee is spent on production.

  3. 03

    Pre-production

    1–2 weeks

    Crew, cast, locations, permits, CGI asset build and a shooting schedule locked with you. This is where overruns are prevented.

  4. 04

    Production & craft

    1–3 weeks

    Shoot days or render passes, animation and compositing. Daily stills or WIP frames shared, so you're never waiting in silence.

  5. 05

    Finish & deliver

    1–2 weeks

    Grade, sound design and mix, two rounds of revisions, then every version, ratio and caption file packaged and handed over.

Yes — a good share of our work is remote. We've delivered for brands and agencies across the Gulf, UK, Europe and North America. Everything runs through shared review links, written approvals and scheduled calls, so distance is a planning detail rather than a problem.

Discovery call in your time zone → written brief and fixed estimate → treatment and storyboard approval → production → two revision rounds → delivery. Payments are milestone-based, usually 50% to begin and the balance on final delivery. We invoice in USD, AED or INR, whichever suits your accounts team.

It depends on what's being made, so we don't publish a rate card — a 30-second CGI product film and a three-day shoot with crew have very different cost structures. Send us the brief and a budget range and you'll get a line-itemed estimate, usually within two working days. We'd rather quote accurately than cheaply and renegotiate later.

A typical two-to-three minute film takes four to eight weeks from brief to final delivery. Animation and CGI-heavy work sits toward the longer end because render and iteration time is real. If your date is fixed, tell us at the start — we'll either commit to it or say no early.

Both. We can shoot on the ground in Kerala and the UAE with our own crew and gear, or run a hybrid production where live plates are combined with CGI and animation. If a shoot is happening in a city we're not in, we'll produce it remotely with a vetted local crew and direct over a live feed.

You do, on final payment — full usage rights to the finished work, plus the master files and project archives on request. Third-party assets such as licensed music or stock are transferred under their own licence terms, which we document for you.
